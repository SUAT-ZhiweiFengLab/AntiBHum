# Manuscript figures

These files are the original embedded JPEG figures extracted from the maintainer-supplied `Combined_Manuscript.pdf`, titled *AntiBHum: Integrating Template-Guided Design and Experiment-Derived Residue Constraints for Antibody Humanization* (Cong Liu, Ouyang Mo, Gavin Hou, et al.). No published DOI or public manuscript URL was supplied.

The images were extracted directly from PDF image objects, without redrawing, cropping, resampling, or JPEG recompression. PDF page numbers below count from the first page, including the title page. The complete manuscript is not included in this repository.

| File | Manuscript figure | PDF page | Dimensions | Content |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| [figure1.jpg](figure1.jpg) | Figure 1 | 6 | 1207 × 1245 px | Residue protection and rank-matched V/J template workflow |
| [figure2.jpg](figure2.jpg) | Figure 2 | 8 | 1214 × 1233 px | Antibody organization and the 329-parent computational comparison |
| [figure3.jpg](figure3.jpg) | Figure 3 | 9 | 2876 × 1833 px | Nanobody framework humanization and Nano300 mean scores |

The README captions are concise descriptions based on the manuscript captions and accompanying Results/Methods. Quantitative statements are attributed to the manuscript, not new analyses performed by this repository. Figure 2 shows 329 common evaluable parents from the 339-parent curated set; Figure 3 uses 300 nanobody sequences. Figure 1 illustrates five candidates; the CLI default is nine templates per chain.
